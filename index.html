What is this showing?

The simulation separates geometry from topology.

01

Non-orientable

A Möbius strip has no globally consistent front/back orientation. Follow a center path once and your local orientation flips.

02

One boundary

The two apparent long edges are actually one continuous boundary curve after the half-twist and join.

03

Center cut

Cutting a one-half-twist Möbius strip along its center produces one longer band rather than two separate bands.

04

Repeat the experiment

The resulting band has four half-twists. A later center cut can split an even-twist band into two linked bands with the same twist count.

This browser simulation generates its ribbon geometry procedurally. The recursive cut sequence follows standard Möbius-strip topology rules; the UI limits how many rendered pieces are shown at once for mobile performance.
